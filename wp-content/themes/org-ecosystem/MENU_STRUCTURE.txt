# Organization Ecosystem - Recommended Menu Structure

Follow this structure when setting up your WordPress Menus for the best user experience.

---

## 1. Primary Navigation (The Main Menu)
*Target:* All visitors and logged-in users.

1.  **Home**
2.  **About Us** (Dropdown)
    *   Our Mission
    *   Governance & Leadership
    *   Partner With Us
3.  **Member Directory** (The main [org_directory] page)
4.  **Marketplace** (The [org_product_grid] page)
5.  **Job Board**
6.  **Events**
7.  **Resources**
8.  **Support Our Mission** (The [org_donation_form] page)
9.  **Contact Us**

---

## 2. Header Action Buttons
*Logged Out:*
*   [Login] - (wp-login.php)
*   [Join Now] - (Link to Join page)

*Logged In:*
*   [Dashboard] - (Link to Member Dashboard)
*   [Logout] - (wp-logout.php)

---

## 3. Footer Navigation
*Target:* Secondary links and legal info.

1.  **News & Updates**
2.  **FAQs**
3.  **Referral Program**
4.  **Privacy Policy**
5.  **Terms & Conditions**
