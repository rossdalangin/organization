# Organization Ecosystem - Administrator & User Guide

A complete guide for managing your organization and navigating the professional dashboard.

---

## 1. For Administrators: The Command Center

### Dashboard Overview
The "Org Ecosystem" menu in your WordPress sidebar is your cockpit. It displays four vital "Vitality Cards":
1.  **Active Base:** Total paid members appearing in the directory.
2.  **Gross Revenue:** Total income from dues and donations.
3.  **Marketplace:** Total products listed by members.
4.  **Support Load:** Open inquiries from your community.

### Management Workflows
#### Approving a New Member
1.  Receive a notification of a new registration.
2.  Go to **Org Ecosystem > Membership**.
3.  Find the applicant under **Pending Applications**.
4.  Click **Approve & Activate**.
5.  *Result:* The member receives an approval email, and their profile becomes visible in the public directory.

#### Customizing Homepage Sections
The homepage is modular. You can enable/disable sections and edit their content via **Appearance > Customize > 2. Homepage & Layout**.

#### Adding Events & Announcements
1.  Go to **Events > Add New**.
2.  Fill in the date, time, and location.
3.  Add a featured image and description.
4.  *Result:* The event will automatically appear in the "Upcoming Events" section of the homepage.

## 2. For Members: The Professional Dashboard

### Navigating Your Hub
Your dashboard is divided into specialized tabs for business growth:
*   **Insights:** View your profile impressions and product clicks.
*   **Profile & Bio:** Edit your "mini-website" (Bio, Cover Photo, Social Links).
*   **My Offerings:** Add, edit, or delete your products and services.
*   **Referral Center:** Get your unique code to earn 10% on community growth.
*   **Communication:** Send/receive direct messages and join the Community Group Chat.

### Professional Workflows
#### Promoting Your Business
1.  Go to **My Offerings**.
2.  Click the **Promote** button next to your best product.
3.  Complete the checkout for the **Featured Listing Fee**.
4.  *Result:* Your product will now appear in the "Featured Solutions" section on the homepage for increased visibility.

#### Managing Your Product Listings
1.  Go to **My Offerings > Add New Product**.
2.  Enter title, description, price, and category.
3.  Upload high-quality product images.
4.  *Result:* Your product is now searchable in the marketplace and on your member profile.

## 3. Communication & Networking

### Direct Messaging
Members can message each other directly from profile pages.
*   **Use Case:** A Corporate member finds a Vendor in the directory and sends a "Project Inquiry" through the dashboard messaging system.

### Community Group Chat
The **Community Chat** tab in the dashboard provides a real-time area for all members to discuss trends, share opportunities, and ask for help.

---

## 4. Financial Management

### Wallet & Commissions
If you refer a member who joins or sells a product, you earn a **10% Referral Commission**.
*   **Tracking:** View your balance in the **Earnings & Ledger** tab.
*   **Withdrawal:** Once you reach the minimum threshold (e.g., ₱500.00), click **Request Payout** in the Financial Center.

---

## 5. Support & Troubleshooting

### Submitting a Ticket
1.  Go to the **Support** tab in your dashboard.
2.  Click **Open New Ticket**.
3.  Describe your issue and attach screenshots if necessary.
4.  *Result:* An administrator will respond to your ticket through the dashboard system.
