# Recommended Header and Footer Menu Structure

## 1. Header Menu (Primary Navigation)
Goal: High visibility, easy access to core ecosystem features.

### A. General Public / Guest View
- Home (Icon: House)
- Directory (Icon: People) - *Link to /directory*
- Businesses (Icon: Shop) - *Link to /businesses archive*
- Events (Icon: Calendar) - *Link to /events archive*
- Resources (Icon: File-PDF) - *Link to /resources archive*
- Join Now (Button Style) - *Link to /join*
- Login (Icon: Lock) - *Link to /dashboard or /login*

### B. Logged-in Member View
- Dashboard (Icon: Speedometer)
- Directory (Icon: People)
- Messaging (Icon: Chat)
- Community Chat (Icon: People-Fill)
- Logout (Icon: Box-Arrow-Right)

### C. Admin / Staff View
- Command Center (Link to wp-admin Settings)
- Member Management
- Reports
- Support Inbox

---

## 2. Footer Menu (Utility & Engagement)
Goal: Secondary links, SEO, and organizational information.

### Column 1: Organization
- About Us
- Our Mission
- Governance / Board
- Contact Us

### Column 2: Ecosystem
- Member Directory
- Business Showcase
- Product Marketplace
- Job Board

### Column 3: Support & Legal
- FAQ
- Support Tickets
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- GDPR / Data Export

### Column 4: Revenue & Growth
- Membership Plans
- Donation Page
- Referral Program
- Partner With Us

---

## Navigation Logic Implementation
- Use the `org_ecosystem_filter_nav_menu` function in `inc/template-functions.php` to conditionally show/hide these items based on login status.
- Add CSS classes like `menu-item-join` or `logged-in-only` in the WordPress Menu Editor to trigger these visibility rules.
